# Simpl.weather custom image replacement

14 transparent PNG originals supplied by Dave, copied byte for byte on 2026-09-15. They fill the 16 day/night custom weather slots in slot-map.json. Snow and fog share their day images at night.

This is an image-source replacement only. The original widget layout, text, fonts, symbols, tap actions and weather data remain as supplied.

The slot order was verified against Widgy Customize: day clear, fair/partly cloudy, cloudy, light rain, heavy rain, heavy rain and thunder, snow, fog; then the same sequence for night.

The provided cloudy artwork includes a small sun or moon. The fog artwork is the provided beige cloud with horizontal mist lines. These are the closest supplied variants; the artwork was not modified.
